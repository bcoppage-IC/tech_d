# Risk Register -- [Team / Project] -- Sprint 2 Demo

List at least three risks. For each: probability, impact, a specific
mitigation, and an owner. Open a backlog issue for each risk and link
it in the Issue column.

Probability / Impact scale: Low | Medium | High

| # | Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation | Owner | Issue |
|---|------|-------------|--------|------------|-------|-------|
| 1 | Laptop dies   | Low | High | Ensure that machine is fully charged | B. Coppage| 2 |
| 2 | API doesn't start | Medium | High | Restart API | R. Hall | 3 |
| 3 | App during demo | Medium | high | Acknowledge and pivot to Q and A | Team | 1 |

## Notes
- Make risks specific (e.g. "live API returns 500 during the demo"),
  not generic ("something breaks").
- A good mitigation is an action someone can take now or on demo day.
- Advanced: add systemic risks and mitigations such as backups,
  monitoring, and rollback.
