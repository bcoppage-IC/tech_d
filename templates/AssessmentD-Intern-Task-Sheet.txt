========================================================================
TASK 1 -- RISK REGISTER
========================================================================
"List at least three risks that could affect your Sprint 2 demo. For
each, provide a mitigation strategy and an owner."

Use the risk_register.md template. Columns:
  Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation | Owner

Aim for this:
  - 3 or more specific, realistic risks (not "something might break").
  - Each risk has a concrete mitigation and a named owner.
  - Open a backlog issue for each risk and link it.

You're done when:
  - risk_register.md is filled in and committed, and each risk links
    to an issue in your repo.


========================================================================
TASK 2 -- RELEASE PLAN
========================================================================
System context: a simple system -- [API] + [DB] + [UI].

"Write a release plan for deploying this system. Include pre-flight
checks, step-by-step release, rollback steps, and monitoring signals
(health checks, error rate, latency). Add who does what during
incidents and how to escalate."

Use the release_plan.md and incident_log.md templates in ops/.

Aim for this:
  - A clear pre-flight checklist.
  - Ordered, numbered release steps.
  - A SPECIFIC rollback trigger (what makes you roll back) + the steps.
  - Monitoring signals with real numbers: health check, error-rate
    threshold, latency target.
  - Who does what during an incident, and how to escalate.

You're done when:
  - ops/release_plan.md covers preflight, steps, rollback, monitoring,
    and roles/escalation, and ops/incident_log.md has a template or
    one example entry.
